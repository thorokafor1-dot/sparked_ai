**Editor's note on tier placements:** the S/A/B/C/D/F assignments below are the **ranking from `ideation_best_places_tier_list.md`, corrected against the creator's real results** (gym, dance social, nightclub, restaurant, and beach placements all reflect real-experience feedback, not generic community consensus). Every location segment is written as a self-contained, reorderable block (tier label, location name, footage cue, verdict) specifically so any block can be cut and moved to a different tier position without rewriting surrounding copy if the creator corrects the order again. If you move a location, also update its on-screen tier-board cue and the HOOK/TEASE/RECAP lines that reference specific locations by name (currently: Restaurant in TEASE, Bar/Nightclub/Dance Social in the HOOK footage note, Restaurant/Bar/Nightclub/Dance Social in the RECAP).

**Footage note:** the concept doc's Footage Plan only specifies "pull your existing infield clips, one representative approach per location," no specific clip filenames or timestamps are given. FOOTAGE cues below are therefore written as selection criteria for the editor, not references to a specific existing clip. Per this channel's no-fabricated-dialogue rule, no line of dialogue from any woman in the footage is written or quoted anywhere in this script, her reactions are described directionally only, and the actual footage carries the specific moment.

**Live tier board note:** the board is **built live on screen during recording, not a premade finished graphic**. Every ON-SCREEN cue below that says "drag the tile" assumes you're screen-recording a real tier-list tool (e.g. tiermaker.com) while narrating, dragging each location's tile into its row in real time as you deliver that segment's verdict line, the reveal of where a location lands should feel like it's happening live, not like cutting to an already-finished animation. Recording setup: screen-record the board full-screen (or as a large panel) with your webcam as a picture-in-picture, so viewers see both your reaction and the drag happening at once, edit the talking-head cutaways in around that screen recording rather than replacing it. Practice the drag placements once before the real take so it reads as confident, not fumbling.

**Visual structure per location segment (3-shot pattern, repeats for all 13 locations):**
1. **Board + webcam bubble** for the setup line (the first sentence or two of the VO, stating the tier and location).
2. **Cut to the real infield approach clip** named in that segment's FOOTAGE line, muted or with ambient/SFX only, no dialogue audio, while the VO continues narrating the pro/con over it. This is the "background clip of yourself doing approaches" for that location, it's the same clip already specified per segment, just cut to full-screen (or large) here instead of staying on the board the whole time.
3. **Cut back to board + webcam bubble** for the tile drag and the VERDICT VO line, so the live drag and the punchline land together.
This pattern is what turns 13 back-to-back "you talking over a static board" segments into something with actual visual movement, don't skip step 2 even on segments where you're tempted to just stay on the board the whole time.
