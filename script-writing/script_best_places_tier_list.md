**Editor's note on tier placements:** the S/A/B/C/D/F assignments below are the **draft ranking from `ideation_best_places_tier_list.md`, not yet confirmed against real results**. Every location segment is written as a self-contained, reorderable block (tier label, location name, footage cue, verdict) specifically so any block can be cut and moved to a different tier position without rewriting surrounding copy if the creator corrects the order. If you move a location, also update its on-screen tier-board cue and the two teaser lines in the HOOK/TEASE blocks that reference "F tier" and "S tier" by name.

FOOTAGE cues below are therefore written as selection criteria for the editor, not references to a specific existing clip. Per this channel's no-fabricated-dialogue rule, no line of dialogue from any woman in the footage is written or quoted anywhere in this script, her reactions are described directionally only, and the actual footage carries the specific moment.
