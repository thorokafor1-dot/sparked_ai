# Video Ideation

Single job of this folder: come up with new video ideas and titles. Two tiers:
1. **Backlog** (`video_ideas.md`) — raw brainstorming: new concept ideas and candidate titles, loosely sorted, not fully planned yet.
2. **Concept docs** (`ideation_<short_name>.md`) — once an idea is worth pursuing, it gets promoted into a full single-video plan: title decision, hook angle, thumbnail direction, pacing/retention plan, footage plan.

Neither tier is reusable pattern research (that's `shorts-hook-research/` and `long-form-hook-research/`) or outlier scoring (that's `outlier-tracking/`) — this folder is about generating and planning *this channel's next videos*.

## Contents
- `video_ideas.md` — running backlog of raw video ideas and title candidates.
- `ideation_10_openers.md` — concept doc for a "10 opening lines" countdown Short: title candidates, thumbnail reference, pacing models from comparable outliers, footage plan.
- `thumbnail_comparison.png` — thumbnail research reference image for that concept.

## Doc pattern
Each promoted video concept gets its own `ideation_<short_name>.md` file with:
- **Concept** — one-line description of the video.
- **Title Candidates** — options with reasoning, and a decision if one's been made.
- **Thumbnail Reference** — local image or published board link, plus the winning pattern from comparable channels.
- **Model Videos** — title/views/channel table of comparable outliers to draw pacing/structure from.
- **Pacing / Retention Models** — breakdown of how 1-2 comparable videos are structured, applied back to this video.
- **Footage Plan** — what existing footage/talent covers this concept.
- **Open Questions / Next Steps** — checklist of what's left before this goes into production.

## Scope rules
- Pulls model videos from `outlier-tracking/` data and pattern notes from `shorts-hook-research/` / `long-form-hook-research/` as reference — doesn't duplicate that research itself.
- Doesn't post, edit, or track outliers — purely ideation/planning docs that feed into `long-form-to-shorts-video-editing/` once a concept is greenlit.
