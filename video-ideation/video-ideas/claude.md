# Video Ideas (by content type)

Standalone idea/research threads that aren't full concept docs yet, organized by production format so a growing backlog stays navigable. Once an idea is ready to promote, it still becomes an `ideation_<name>.md` in the parent `video-ideation/` folder, same as always.

## Subfolders
- `bar/`: bar cold approach, line-testing, location/venue angles, anything shot at a bar.
- `monkey-app-video-chat/`: Monkey App / video-chat format ideas (distinct medium from in-person, don't mix patterns from one into the other without saying so).
- `street-daytime-pov/`: street daytime cold approach, both POV (solo phone/glasses) and third-person cameraman-shot.
- `explainer/`: talking-head/explainer videos about flirting and dating that aren't infield footage at all.
- `general/`: ideas that don't fit one category cleanly, or are meant to apply across multiple formats (e.g. a title pattern that works for both bar and street content). Not a dumping ground, if an idea clearly belongs in one category, file it there instead.

## Scope rules
- Same rules as the parent `video-ideation/claude.md`: pulls model videos from `outlier-tracking/` and pattern notes from `shorts-hook-research/` / `long-form-hook-research/` as reference, doesn't duplicate that research itself, doesn't post or track outliers.
