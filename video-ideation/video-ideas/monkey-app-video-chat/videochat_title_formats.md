# Video Chat E-Date Title Formats

Status: **active**. These are repeatable title formats for the e-date long-forms. Every format has a **model video** that already proved the concept on a comparable channel, plus Sparked's wording of it.

## How the proof is measured
- **Source:** the last 24 long-forms of each direct-inspiration channel (Jameer, Jay Throck, Zaen Azzuri, ItsMP3, LilPraisey, Edtki, Teige), pulled 2026-10-09 with `thumbnail-creation/pull_channel_thumbs.py`, plus the Video Chat rows of `outlier-tracking/niche-long-form/data.json`.
- **x = times that channel's own median views.** This compares like with like, so a 32K-sub channel and a 340K-sub channel can be read side by side.
- **Celebrity and streamer cameos are excluded** (IShowSpeed, Clavicular, Kai Cenat collabs). Their clicks come from the famous name, not the format.

## Wording rules (apply to every format)
1. **Lead with the keywords: "Flirting" and "Women" (or "Girls").** This is the formula your second channel already proved ("Flirting with women is ___", "___ to flirt with women"). The keywords carry search and suggested traffic, and the format carries the click.
2. **Say "e-dates", never name the app** (brand rule).
3. **Women/girls, not "baddies".** The genre leans on "baddies", but the audience is 18-35 men and the brand is mature.
4. **Statements, not questions to the viewer.** No smirk emoji. One emoji at most, if any.
5. **Never put words in her mouth, and never promise a result the video doesn't show.**

**Best bets for Sparked:**
- **#4 Wingman** has the strongest brand fit, because it *is* teaching flirting, and it has 5.9x proof.
- **#2 Bingo** has the highest ceiling.
- **#1 Best-of** is the most reliable: every inspiration channel's top video is a compilation.
- **#8 Seasonal is time-sensitive:** Halloween is 2026-10-31, three weeks out. Both of Edtki's Halloween videos beat his median.

**Series rule:** formats 3, 4 and 9 are series by design. When an episode beats the channel median, make "Pt. 2" next (Jameer's helping-viewers series went Pt.1 4.6x, then Pt.2 2.0x).

## Proven to underperform (don't use as the main format)
- **Single-moment "SHE ..." story claims** ("SHE CAME OVER AT 3AM", "SHE WAS IN MY CITY AND TOLD ME TO PULL UP", "They BEGGED Me To Come Over"): Edtki's average about 1.0x, Jay 0.58x, Teige 0.64x. They also tend to put words or intent in her mouth.
- **"After dark" / "3AM" time-of-day** on its own: Jay "Monkey App After Dark..." 0.64x. It only worked for MarcusT as part of a numbered series.
- **Generic "How to rizz"**: ItsMP3 0.44x, Jay 0.76x. It varies too much, and works only as a best-of in disguise.
