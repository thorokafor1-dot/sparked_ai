# Video Idea, Bar Line-Testing Recurring Format

Status: **active/recurring**. You go to the bar weekly and test lines on camera, this format is built to consume that footage indefinitely rather than being a one-off video.

## Recommended format
Episodic tier-list/ranking series: one video per bar night (or batched multi-night), you test several lines on different women, each line gets ranked by her actual real reaction.

**Title formula:** `I Tested [N] Pickup Lines At The Bar (Ranked By Her Reaction)`

## Two backup cuts (same weekly footage, different edit)
- **"One line, one reaction" mini-series**: `I Said This At The Bar... Her Reaction` per video, one clip = one episode, highest output rate for burning through a weekly backlog.
- **"Best of" compilation**: `Every Pickup Line I've Tested At The Bar, Ranked`, periodic recap tier-list built from the full library, same structure as `ideation_best_places_tier_list.md`'s script, swap locations for lines.
