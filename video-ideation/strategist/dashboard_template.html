Making the thumbnail before you shoot

  1. Open an idea, expand its thumbnail plan and copy the prompt.
  2. In Grok Imagine, upload your reference photo, paste the prompt and generate. Check that the man really looks like you.
  3. Every prompt puts a woman in the main position. This is a concept mockup, so shoot toward the same framing and build the final thumbnail from a real frame.
  4. Send the image back and it goes through the crop, caption and quality checks.
