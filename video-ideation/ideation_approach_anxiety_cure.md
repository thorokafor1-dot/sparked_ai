# Video Ideation, "The Number 1 Cure for Approach Anxiety"

## Concept
A long-form talking-head + infield video making one argument: there is no trick that removes approach anxiety first. The only cure is to face the fear, approach anyway, and let enough real approaches teach your nervous system that nothing bad happens. Infield footage is the proof on screen.

## Title Candidates
- "How I Cured My Approach Anxiety" (Casey Zander / Benjamin Seda / Austen Summers framing, first person)
- "The #1 Cure for Approach Anxiety (It's Not What You Want to Hear)"
- "How to Overcome Approach Anxiety (For Good)" (Orion Taraban / Coach Kyle framing)

**Decision: "How I Cured My Approach Anxiety."** This exact first-person framing is the one used by three of the six model videos (Casey Zander 217K, Benjamin Seda 68K, Austen Summers 75K), including the single highest-viewed direct analog in the model set. It also matches the vulnerability/stakes-setting archetype (Type E in `long-form-hook-research/infield/hook_patterns.md`) the hook below is built around, a personal-cure claim sets up the "prove it to yourself" frame the footage then resolves. Keeps "approach anxiety" in the title per the brand rule, no dance-request angle.

## Thumbnail Reference
- Winning patterns from the model set (thumbnails pulled 2026-09-30):
  - **Infield moment, woman as focal point** (Casey Zander, Coach Kyle, The Attractive Man). Safest, matches the channel rule that a woman is the main draw.
  - **Before/after split, "Day 1 vs Day 5"** (Austen Summers, 75K). Left: you hesitating / nervous. Right: mid-conversation, her smiling. Fits "face the fear" as a visible transformation.
- Avoid: creator-solo quote thumbnails (Todd V style) and illustrated/AI art (Hamza, Orion). Neither fits the brand rule.

## Pacing / Retention Models
Structure read from the full transcripts (fetched 2026-09-30).

### Model 1: Casey Zander (~8 min, the user's structure pick)
1. Promise in line one ("how I actually cured my approach anxiety").
2. "Who this is for" call-out, then his own backstory of struggling to talk to anyone.
3. Lists the excuses guys make in the moment (walking too fast, headphones, bad timing). Very relatable, the viewer sees himself.
4. Epiphany: everyone feels this way, everyone wants connection.
5. Core fix stated as a principle, then the honest cost: "my internals were burning", cringing inside while doing it.
6. Payoff: who you become in the process, peace of mind vs. regret.
7. CTA (webinar), like/subscribe.

**Apply:** use the promise + call-out + excuse list as the first third. The excuse list is the strongest retention beat.

### Model 2: AskToddV (~6 min)
1. Two methods (jump in / gradual desensitization), then the real answer.
2. Thought experiment: if someone forced you, you'd approach. So the fear isn't a capability problem, it's a choice problem.
3. "Thou shalt approach": he made approaching a non-negotiable rule, removing the choice.
4. Personal story as proof (college tour turned into approaching everyone).

**Apply:** the "remove the choice" rule is a concrete, repeatable action to give the viewer (e.g. a 3-second rule, "if I notice her, I go").

### Model 3: Orion Taraban (~8 min, the user's message pick)
1. "The way to do it is to do it." Videos, books and coaching don't move the needle, only approaches do.
2. Mechanism: each approach is an emotionally disconfirming experience; rejection becomes "just information".
3. Take massive action: a handful of approaches a week can take years to reach the threshold.
4. Second half: acting reframe (you're already imagining a negative scene, so imagine a confident one).

**Apply:** this is the thesis. Explain the mechanism in plain words (fear shrinks through exposure, not preparation), then the massive-action push. Keep the acting reframe out or as a short bonus so the message stays single-minded. Don't reuse his client anecdote as ours.

### Combined arc for this video
Hook (the cure is the thing you're avoiding) → call-out + excuse list → why it's fear, not skill → the cure: face it, with the exposure mechanism → the rule that removes choice → infield proof → who you become → CTA.

## Hook Plan
Built from `long-form-hook-research/infield/hook_patterns.md` (this video is talking-head-led but opens and proves its claim on real infield footage, so the infield archetypes apply to the hook window). Combines three archetypes from that doc: **Type A** (zero-setup cold open, no branding, the single highest-scoring pattern in the dataset), **Type E** (vulnerability/self-deprecating stakes-setting), and the call-out beat from Model 1 (Casey Zander) in the Pacing/Retention Models above. Per that doc's cross-cutting observations, no subscribe ask goes in this window, long-form in this niche holds the ask until after the first payoff.

Footage note: the cold open needs a real clip from `long-form-video-editing/talking-head-infield/input/infield/` showing visible hesitation before an approach. Exact clip/timestamp is an editing decision, not specified here, since none of these raw clips have been reviewed for this purpose yet. No dialogue is written for any woman in the footage; her presence isn't needed for this specific beat (it's a pre-approach hesitation moment, before contact).

**0:00-0:05, COLD OPEN (infield footage, mic audio only, no logo/branding, no music)**
Visual: real infield clip, a visible hesitation moment before an approach (clip selected at edit time from `input/infield/`).
Spoken line (VO over the clip): "This is the part nobody puts in the video. Not the approach. The five seconds right before it."

**0:05-0:10, hard cut to talking head, straight to camera**
Spoken line: "That feeling is approach anxiety. And there is exactly one thing that cures it." **(thesis/promise stated by 0:10, matches Orion Taraban's "the way to do it is to do it" message)**

**0:10-0:16, talking head continues**
Spoken line: "Not a script. Not a line. Not a video that hypes you up for ten minutes and then leaves you standing there. The cure is that you go anyway, on purpose, before you feel ready."

**0:16-0:22, vulnerability/stakes-setting beat (Type E)**
Spoken line: "[PLACEHOLDER: your own approach-anxiety low point, when it was worst and roughly how many approaches it took before it eased, per the Open Questions below. Do not fill this with an invented story.]"

**0:22-0:27, call-out beat (bridges directly into Model 1's excuse list, the next beat in the Combined Arc)**
Spoken line: "If you've ever made up an excuse in the two seconds before you could've said something, walked too fast, told yourself she's probably busy, decided the moment already passed, this is for you."

**0:27-0:30, escalation tease (curiosity gap into the body)**
Spoken line: "And by the end of this I'll give you the one rule that makes sure you never talk yourself out of it again." (tees up AskToddV's "remove the choice" rule without revealing it yet)

**Subscribe ask:** not in this window. Per the infield pattern doc, place it after the first infield proof clip lands later in the video (after the excuse list / mechanism section), not before the first payoff.

**List/order tie-in:** the 0:22-0:27 call-out line is the direct handoff into the Combined Arc's next beat (call-out + excuse list), so the hook doesn't resolve the tension itself, it hands off cleanly into Casey Zander's excuse-list structure that follows.
