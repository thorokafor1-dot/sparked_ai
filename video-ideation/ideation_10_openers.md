## Pacing / Retention Models

### Model 1: "10 Pick Up Lines that Spark Attraction & Actually Work" (The Attractive Man, 1.64M views, ~6 min)
Exact topic match, literally a countdown of 10 lines.
- Cold open mid-line (no preamble) as the hook, brand intro follows at ~0:13
- Subscribe ask early (~0:32), before the list starts
- **Descending countdown, 10 → 1** (biggest/best line saved for last, classic countdown-show convention)
- Each item (~30–35 sec avg): state the line verbatim → explain *why* it works psychologically → a usage caveat/context note → occasional personal anecdote for texture
- Ends on a lead-magnet CTA (free "conversation cheat sheet"), not just subscribe

### Model 2: "Signs That A Girl Likes You!" (JJEverettRose, 1.2M views, Nov 2025, ~16 min)
Not cold-approach, but the best recent example of retention mechanics in this broader niche.
- Hook (0:13–0:30) makes an **explicit escalation promise**: "we'll start obvious, end with genius-level insight", this single line is why a long list doesn't feel like a slog
- Every item runs the same **~15–20 sec micro-loop**: stat/hook line → the fact → one-line "why" → personal reaction/joke → next. No lingering, no re-explaining.
- A single running mechanic (descending "X% of people know this" counter, 98% → 0.5%) acts as a progress bar for the whole video, sustains curiosity across a long runtime
- Humor/reaction after *nearly every item*, not just the funny ones

### Applied to your video
- State the ordering logic in the hook (e.g., "ranked safest to boldest" or "lowest to highest response rate"), gives 10 items a reason to escalate instead of reading as a flat list
- Cap each opener at ~20–30 sec: state the line → cut to real footage → one-line result/reaction → next
- Attach a stat or rank to every opener individually (not just the video concept), that's the actual retention mechanism in both models, more than the content itself
- Subscribe ask early, not just at the end (both models do this)
