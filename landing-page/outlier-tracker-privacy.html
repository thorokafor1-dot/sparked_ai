Outlier Tracker — Data Usage & Privacy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This page is separate from Sparked's client-facing coaching brand and privacy policy. It describes a private, internal research tool ("Outlier Tracker") that is not a public product, not offered to coaching clients, and not related to any client-facing service.

1. What This Tool Is

Outlier Tracker is a private, internal dashboard used only by our own team to study publicly available YouTube video packaging (titles, thumbnails, view counts) across a research niche, for the sole purpose of informing our own content planning.

2. YouTube API Data Usage

This tool uses the YouTube Data API to retrieve publicly available data: video titles, thumbnails, channel names, and statistics such as view and subscriber counts. This data is used strictly for internal research and is never sold, redistributed, or shown to the public or to any end users outside our own team.

3. Retention and Deletion

Video-level data such as titles and channel names is refreshed or deleted within 30 days of retrieval. Publicly available statistics and any metrics we derive from them (such as performance scores) may be retained for longer, up to 36 months, consistent with YouTube's API Services policies.

4. Terms

Our use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and by using this tool we agree to be bound by the Google Privacy Policy.
