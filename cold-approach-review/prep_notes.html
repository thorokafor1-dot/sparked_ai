Patterns to watch tonight

the same ones kept showing up
01

You close the interaction yourself the second it gets skeptical or flat. "Have a good day" is your default exit even when there was somewhere to take it. Reframe or ask a curiosity follow-up before you bail.

02

You ask for the number after the energy already dropped, not at the peak. Belina in 00036: good rapport on "where are you from," then it went flat, then you asked for Instagram into dead air. Ask right when it's warm.

03

You're avoiding the close itself, not just fumbling it. On tape: "I keep my phone far away so it's not recording," and "I never put her Instagram in my phone, because then they can just ignore me." That's the fear talking before the ask even happens. Ask anyway.

04

You run out of material for the second girl in a group. Good with the first, then admit on tape you're "stuck," "it's boring," nothing left to say. Have one dedicated question ready just for the friend before you need it.

05

Names don't survive the day. You flagged this yourself as a real problem. Voice memo the name right after you walk away, every time.

06

Indirect bits land flat. The fortune-teller / lifesaver line confused more than it charmed, Nikos's own note was "you have to make it obvious." Cut the setup, land the point sooner.

07

Age-check early in group approaches. One case where you didn't clock a 16 year old in the group until after. Build it into the first ten seconds.
